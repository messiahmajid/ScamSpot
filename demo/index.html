ScamSpot Demo

Sample messages with a mix of phishing and legitimate links. ScamSpot should flag the red section and leave the green section alone.

How to use this demo 1. Load the ScamSpot extension in Chrome (extension-dist/ folder)
2. Open this page at http://localhost:8080
3. Watch links get scanned automatically
4. Click a flagged link to see the warning overlay
5. Click the ScamSpot toolbar icon to see scan stats
